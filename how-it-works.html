How it works

Set up once. Train every day.

Four steps from unboxing your adapter to running your first stage.

  1. 1

    Fit a lens adapter

    Place it over your scope’s front lens so you can focus on a screen just a few metres away.

    Don't have one yet? Find a lens adapter Buy one in the USA or South Africa, or download free files to 3D-print your own. Free DIY See where to get one
  2. 2

    Calibrate your screen

    Measure the orange line with a ruler and enter its length. Then check the square on screen isn't stretched.

  3. 3

    Set your distance

    Measure from the screen to the front of the scope and enter it in the app. Targets now subtend their true angle in your reticle.

  4. 4

    Build a stage and train

    Build the stage, read the briefing, then shoot it against the par time.

Dry fire only. Ensure the rifle and magazine are unloaded.
