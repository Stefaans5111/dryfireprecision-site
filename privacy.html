Privacy policy

DRYFIRE has no accounts and collects no names, emails or location.

Stored on your device

Settings, ranges, targets and calibration are stored only on your device. Your own range photos stay on your phone.

Purchases

Purchases go through Google Play. Our purchase provider, RevenueCat, uses an anonymous app user ID to know whether Full Access was bought. We never see your payment details.

What leaves the app

Nothing is sent from the app unless you choose to copy or email diagnostics.

Contact
