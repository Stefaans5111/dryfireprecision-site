1. Summary

We built this app to collect as little information as possible.

  • No account is needed. We don't ask for your name, email address or phone number to use the app.
  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics that track how you use the app.
  • Your settings, training data and photos stay on your device.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play (and Apple, on iOS).

2. Information stored on your device only

The app stores the following on your phone or tablet so it works the way you set it up. It is not sent to us:

  • Screen calibration and viewing distance
  • Your selected props and equipment
  • Range layouts, stages and training history
  • Photos you've chosen as range backgrounds
  • App settings and preferences
  • Trial start dates and unlock status (to allow one free trial per device, a hashed device ID is also checked with our purchase provider; see section 5)

Uninstalling the app deletes this information.

3. Photos

To create custom ranges, you can choose a photo from your device to use as a range background.

  • You choose which photo to use. The app only accesses the photos you select, not your whole gallery.
  • Selected photos are stored on your device only, inside the app.
  • Photos are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • Removing a custom range, or uninstalling the app, deletes the app's copy of the photo. Your original photo stays in your gallery.

4. Diagnostics

The app has an About & Diagnostics screen that shows technical details (such as the app version and purchase status) to help with support. This information is never sent automatically. It only leaves your device if you choose to copy it or email it to us.

5. Purchases and free trials

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play (or the Apple App Store on iOS). We never receive or store your card or banking details.

To check whether you've bought a feature and to restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives:

  • your purchase history for this app (which products you bought and when)
  • an anonymous app user ID, which doesn't include your name or email address
  • a one-way hashed device ID, created from your device's Android ID. The raw Android ID is never sent. It is used only to allow one free trial per device.

This information is used only to unlock what you've paid for. It isn't used for advertising.

  • Google Play privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

6. If you contact us

If you choose to contact us by email, WhatsApp or the contact form on this website, for example to ask a question, order a scope adapter, or ask to be notified about new features, we receive the details you send us, such as your name, phone number, email address and message.

  • We use them only to reply to you or to do what you asked (for example, notify you once when a feature launches).
  • We don't sell or share them for marketing.
  • You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Messages sent through the website contact form are delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms, a form-delivery service (web3forms.com/privacy).

7. What we do not do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't show ads or use ad tracking.
  • We don't access your location, contacts, camera or microphone. We only access the specific photos you choose for range backgrounds.
  • We don't use your information to build profiles about you.

8. Children

This app is intended for adults (18+) who take part in precision rifle shooting. It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Your rights (POPIA)

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct or delete it
  • object to how it's used
  • lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za)

To make a request, email dryfireapp@gmail.com. We'll reply within a reasonable time.

10. Security

Information stored on your device is protected by your device's own security. Any information you send us directly is kept only as long as needed and is stored securely.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes, for example if we add new features. The latest version will always be on this page, with the effective date at the top.
