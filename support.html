Support

How can we help?

How do I calibrate my screen?+

Measure the orange line on the calibration screen with a ruler and enter its length. Then check the square isn’t stretched. Calibrate each device you use.

What distance should I enter?+

Measure from the screen to the front of the scope with a tape measure and enter that. Target sizes depend on this distance: the closer the number in the app is to the real distance, the more precisely the targets are scaled. If the distance is 10% out, target sizes are about 10% out too. Confirm the distance before each session, and measure again if you move your setup.

The targets look blurry in my scope.+

Check your lens adapter is fitted, then adjust the parallax/focus and the distance until the plate edges are sharp.

Why does the picture look "blocky" or pixelated through my scope?+

Your scope magnifies the screen, so at higher magnification you can start to see the screen's individual pixels.

Bigger screens usually look blockier. Most phones and tablets have a similar number of pixels. On a bigger screen those pixels are spread out more, so each one is larger. The scope magnifies them, and targets and edges can look slightly stepped or blocky.

The app is optimised for:

  • Medium-sized phones: about 6.1" to 6.7"
  • Medium-sized tablets: about 10" to 11"

Larger tablets, monitors and TVs still work and stay true to scale, but the picture may look less sharp through the scope.

To get a sharper picture:

  1. Turn your scope's magnification down. Lower magnification makes the pixels less visible.
  2. Use a device with a high-resolution screen. More pixels per inch gives a smoother picture.
  3. When casting to a TV, remember that Full HD (1080p) is the maximum, so a big TV will look blockier than a phone or tablet.
  4. Check the focus. If the picture looks blurry rather than blocky, the problem is focus, not pixels. Check your scope lens adapter and parallax setting.

This doesn't affect accuracy. Target sizes stay true to scale on every screen once you've calibrated. Only the sharpness changes.

Sign in to the same Google account, then open Settings → Restore purchases.
