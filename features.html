Features

Everything a match puts in your scope.

Plates, KYL racks and movers at true angular size, on realistic range photos, in real conditions.

1

True-size targets

Calibrate the screen once, enter your distance, and every target is drawn at its true angular size. A 1 MIL plate covers exactly 1 MIL on your reticle.

  • ✓MIL, MOA, inches or mm
  • ✓Ranges in metres or yards
2

Realistic range photos

Targets stand on realistic photos of shooting ranges at real distances, with posts, shadows and markers, so the sight picture looks like a match. Add photos of your own range too.

  • ✓Realistic range photos
  • ✓Add your own photos to create custom ranges
  • ✓Posts, shadows and markers
3

Target types

Everything you meet on a PRS course, plus animal silhouettes for the hunters.

  • ✓Steel plates: circle, square, diamond, IPSC
  • ✓Animals: coyote, bison, wild boar, ground hog
  • ✓Movers at a set speed
  • ✓KYL racks: 12" 10" 8" 4" 2" plates
4

Markers & textures

Label every target the way your match does, and finish the steel the way it really looks. Set shape, size and distance on the built-in keypad.

  • ✓Markers 1 2 3 or A B C
  • ✓As labels or ID boards
  • ✓Painted, rusted or impact textures
5

Stage builder

Choose the range, position heights from prone to standing with props you own, target types, number of targets and positions, difficulty and par time. DRYFIRE writes the full briefing and sets the stage up with one tap. Stages follow PRS club-match practice, adapted for dry fire.

  • ✓Tripod, step ladder, crates and more
  • ✓ROOKIE, AMATEUR, PRO, ELITE
  • ✓Start, positions, course of fire, round count
6

Weather & mirage

Train the conditions, not just the hold.

  • ✓Sun glare with backlit targets and shadows
  • ✓Fog and mist that thicken with distance
  • ✓Heat mirage that makes plate edges shimmer

Train anywhere.

7

Phone or tablet

Train on your phone or tablet. Each display keeps its own calibration.

8

Fully offline

Works completely offline. No account needed.
