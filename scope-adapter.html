Build your own

The video below is a great walkthrough that covers everything you need to build your own adapter: parts, measurements and assembly.

Parts list

  • Flip-up scope cap (Butler Creek type) Must match your scope's bell size View on Amazon
  • Optical lens As used in the video View on Amazon
  • Tape As used in the video View on Amazon

The flip cap must match your scope. Measure the outside of your scope's front bell (not the lens size printed on the scope), then pick the matching cap size from the cap maker's size chart.

Prices shown on Amazon are in your local currency. Building your own may cost more than buying ready-made outside the USA.
